Pharmacy Assistant | Blooms The Chemist Parap

Our Company

Blooms the Chemist is a community-based pharmacy group with a strong national presence. Our network of stores delivers customer care in both metropolitan and rural areas across Australia. We aim to build a future where everyone, everywhere has access to good health and wellbeing.

We value our unique culture, where our people are energetic, resilient and results-driven.

Our Values:

Integrity and Respect

Honest and Trust

Passion and Commitment

The role:

Blooms The Chemist Parap has a fantastic opportunity for a passionate, experienced Pharmacy Assistant that is keen to work with a fun, dynamic and busy team environment! This role will suit anyone friendly, self-motivated and customer-focused.

You will support the pharmacy team by providing a great customer experience for each of our customers, by providing advice and ensuring they receive exceptional service.  

Your role will focus on: 

Actively assisting customers by providing service and product information  

Having an eye for detail and accuracy 

Organizing and maintaining the cleanliness of the store 

Refilling stock, setting up promotions and merchandising displays as required 

Efficient register service 

Completing stock checks 

 
We are looking for someone who is: 

Positive and customer-focused  

A great communicator  

Detail oriented  

Pharmacy experience

What we will give you in return: 

A motivating and rewarding work environment  

Product discounts  

Access to some incredible training and development opportunities
